# Doctor Click: Healthcare App (Flutter)

**A healthcare app to find doctors nearby, book appointments and order medicines from pharmacies close to you.**

Built by [Oriental Tech Solutions](https://github.com/OrientalTechSolutions) · Flutter · iOS & Android

---

## The need

Patients wanted one app to find a doctor nearby, book a visit, and order medicines from a pharmacy close to them.

## What we built

- **Find doctors nearby** on Google Maps, with specialty, rating and availability
- **Appointment booking:** choose a date and time slot, confirm, see upcoming appointments
- **Pharmacy finder:** nearby pharmacies on the map
- **Medicine ordering** with delivery to your door
- **In-app payments**
- **Firebase backend:** authentication, data and notifications

## Tech stack

`Flutter` `Dart` `Firebase` `Google Maps` `Payment gateway` `Push notifications`

## Our role

UI implementation, app development, Firebase integration, maps, payments, testing and store release.
